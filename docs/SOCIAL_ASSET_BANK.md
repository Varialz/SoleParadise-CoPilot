## Art-direction mapping

### Homepage hero
Use one Chrome Hearts campaign frame with strong negative space. Do not rotate hero images automatically.

### From the Feed
Use 6–10 Bottega social frames with inconsistent-but-intentional crops. This should feel closer to a store's Instagram-to-Shopify flow than a polished catalog.

### New Arrivals
Do **not** use campaign/social images here unless they correspond to the exact inventory product. New Arrivals should be actual Shopify product photography.

### Paradise Edit
Use 1 large Chrome campaign image + 1–2 real Shopify products from the same aesthetic family.

### Features
Mix campaign imagery, store photography, and social frames. Each card should have a different visual job; avoid three identical 4:5 cards.

## Production rule

Third-party social/campaign references are for internal mockups, design direction, and temporary client demos. Production should prefer:

1. Sole Paradise-owned photos of actual inventory
2. merchant-supplied assets with permission
3. approved brand campaign imagery where reuse is permitted

Never imply a campaign model is wearing inventory Sole Paradise actually owns unless that exact relationship is verified.
